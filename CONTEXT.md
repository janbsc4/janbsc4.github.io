# Publication

The concepts used to publish and connect the site's writing.

## Language

**Guide**:
A practical post that teaches the reader how to do something. Guides use `post_type: guide`, receive a visible label in post indexes, and are excluded from Read next recommendations.
_Avoid_: Essay, Note

**Related essay**:
A non-hidden Essay manually selected as the closest continuation of the current piece. When none is selected, its recommendation position becomes a discovery essay.
_Avoid_: Closest essay, primary recommendation

**Discovery essay**:
A non-hidden Essay selected at random to encourage exploration beyond the current piece.
_Avoid_: Related essay

**Hidden piece**:
A published Note, Essay, or Guide intentionally excluded from discovery surfaces, including recommendations, while remaining directly accessible.
_Avoid_: Unpublished piece, private piece
