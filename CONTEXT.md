# Publication

The concepts used to publish and connect the site's writing.

## Language

**Related essay**:
The non-hidden essay manually selected as the closest continuation of the current piece. When none is selected, its recommendation position becomes a discovery essay.
_Avoid_: Closest essay, primary recommendation

**Discovery essay**:
A non-hidden essay selected at random to encourage exploration beyond the current piece.
_Avoid_: Related essay

**Hidden piece**:
A published Note or Essay intentionally excluded from discovery surfaces, including recommendations, while remaining directly accessible.
_Avoid_: Unpublished piece, private piece
